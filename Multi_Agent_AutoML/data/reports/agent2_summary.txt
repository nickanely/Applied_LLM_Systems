Final strategy summary

1) Target discovery and schema analysis
- Target column: Churn. Rationale: It is a binary 0/1 variable that directly represents customer attrition and is conventionally the label in telecom churn datasets. It is treated as read-only and was not transformed or encoded.
- Schema: 3333 rows, 11 numeric columns, 0 missing values. Columns: [Churn, AccountWeeks, ContractRenewal, DataPlan, DataUsage, CustServCalls, DayMins, DayCalls, MonthlyCharge, OverageFee, RoamMins].

2) Categorical encoding decision
- Decision: No encoding performed. Although ContractRenewal and DataPlan are binary indicators, they are already numeric (0/1) and well-suited for most models. One-hot/label encoding would be redundant and risks unnecessary collinearity or feature proliferation. The target Churn was never encoded.

3) Interaction features created (from existing columns only)
To capture non-linear effects and domain-relevant relationships, I created the following engineered features:
- interaction_1: DayMins / (DayCalls + 1) — calling intensity (minutes per call), robust to zero calls.
- interaction_2: DataUsage / (AccountWeeks + 1) — normalized data usage per tenure.
- interaction_3: OverageFee / (MonthlyCharge + 1) — overage burden relative to base price.
- interaction_4: RoamMins / (DayMins + 1) — roaming balance within overall voice usage.
- interaction_5: MonthlyCharge + OverageFee — total price pressure.
- interaction_6: CustServCalls * (1 - ContractRenewal) — service-friction when not renewing.
- interaction_7: DataPlan * DataUsage — effective data usage for plan holders.
- interaction_8: DayMins * DataUsage — joint voice-data activity.

All expressions use only existing columns and are numerically stable by adding 1 in denominators where zero is possible.

4) Correlation analysis (with Churn)
Key signals by absolute correlation magnitude with Churn:
- Strongest:
  - ContractRenewal: -0.2599 (customers who renew are less likely to churn)
  - interaction_6 (CustServCalls * (1 - ContractRenewal)): 0.2150
  - CustServCalls: 0.2088
  - DayMins: 0.2052
  - interaction_1 (DayMins / (DayCalls + 1)): 0.1529
- Moderate:
  - DataPlan: -0.1021
  - OverageFee: 0.0928
  - DataUsage: -0.0872
  - interaction_7 (DataPlan * DataUsage): -0.0843
  - interaction_5 (MonthlyCharge + OverageFee): 0.0822
  - interaction_8 (DayMins * DataUsage): -0.0816
  - MonthlyCharge: 0.0723
  - RoamMins: 0.0682
- Low/negligible:
  - interaction_2: -0.0459
  - DayCalls: 0.0185
  - AccountWeeks: 0.0165
  - interaction_4: 0.0080
  - interaction_3: -0.0021

Multicollinearity flags (high inter-feature correlation):
- DataUsage ~ DataPlan: 0.946
- interaction_5 ~ MonthlyCharge: 0.990
- interaction_7 ~ DataUsage: 0.996, and ~ DataPlan: 0.952
- interaction_8 ~ DataUsage: 0.944, ~ DataPlan: 0.892, ~ MonthlyCharge: 0.838, and ~ interaction_7: 0.941

Interpretation: The engineered interaction_6 (service friction) and interaction_1 (usage efficiency) add predictive signal beyond their base components. Several price/usage composites are highly redundant with their base features and should not all be used simultaneously.

5) Feature selection
- Method: Retained the top k=12 most predictive features after feature creation, guided by correlation strength to Churn while recognizing redundancy risks flagged above.
- Evidence-based prioritization: By absolute correlation, the highest-signal candidates are ContractRenewal, interaction_6, CustServCalls, DayMins, interaction_1, DataPlan, OverageFee, DataUsage, interaction_7, interaction_5, interaction_8, and MonthlyCharge (with RoamMins on the margin).
- Redundancy note: Given near-perfect correlation between MonthlyCharge and interaction_5, and the very high correlation of interaction_7 and interaction_8 with DataUsage, downstream modeling should regularize or drop redundant variants to avoid multicollinearity.

What this delivers
- A clean, modeling-ready feature set with targeted interactions that capture behavioral intensity (voice and data), economic pressure (charges/overage), and service friction (support calls relative to renewal), along with a reduced feature subset focused on the strongest signals for Churn.
